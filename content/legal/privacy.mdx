## What this site does not do

This site is a set of static pages. It sets no cookies, uses no analytics or tracking, has no forms or user accounts and embeds no content from other providers, such as videos, maps or social media.

## Data processed when you visit

When you open a page, your browser sends technical data to the web server. The server writes it to a log file:

- the IP address of your device
- the date and time of the request
- the requested address (URL) and the response status
- the amount of data transferred
- the page you came from (referrer)
- your browser and operating system (user agent)

We process this data to deliver the site, to keep it secure and stable and to find errors. The legal basis is Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR: our legitimate interest is the secure and reliable operation of the site. We do not combine this data with other data and do not use it to identify you.

The log file has a fixed maximum size. When it is full, the oldest entries are deleted automatically.

## Fonts and icons

The fonts DM Sans and DM Mono and all icons are part of this site and load from its own server. Your browser does not contact Google or any other font service.

## Data stored on your device

If you choose Light or Dark in the theme setting, your browser stores this choice in its local storage under the key "theme", so the site keeps it on your next visit. The choice never leaves your device. Choose System, or delete the site data in your browser, to remove it.

## Search

The search runs in your browser. It loads index files from this site and looks up your search terms there; your search terms are not sent to any server. The server log records which index files your browser loads, not what you type.

## Links to other websites

Some links lead to other websites: GitHub (also "Give feedback"), Storybook, Figma and npm. Data is sent to these providers only when you click such a link. Their privacy policies apply from then on.

## Your rights

You have the right to:

- get information about the data we process about you (Art. 15 GDPR)
- have incorrect data corrected (Art. 16 GDPR)
- have your data deleted (Art. 17 GDPR)
- have the processing restricted (Art. 18 GDPR)
- receive your data in a common format (Art. 20 GDPR)
- object to the processing of your data based on our legitimate interest (Art. 21 GDPR)

To use these rights, write to [design@med-o.de](mailto:design@med-o.de).
